### What the skin includes
**Player / staff profile and tooltip**
- Media style under personality, best foot and both feet in the tooltip.
- Pros / Cons as coloured badges (like FM24) in the tooltip, the profile and its header.
- Training options (role, focus, intensity), training rating and Praise / Criticise / Training report buttons right
  on the profile.
- Second nationality in the header, Hierarchy and Social group icons next to reputation.
- Staff attribute ratings out of 20, with the same colours as players.

**Club**
- Club header: facility ratings out of 20 with stars, squad personality, finances.
- Reorganised club overview (kits, stadium photo, budgets + finances, legends, affiliations).
- Staff screens like FM24: positions, bar comparison chart, staff table.

**Squad and dynamics**
- Hierarchy as a pyramid with player cards, Social groups as cards.
- Mindset tab like FM24 (coloured cells), adjusted dynamics overview.
- Morale and happiness as icons only in tables, Personality column available in "Insert column".

**Training**
- Training calendar: one button per club team; Training button on the Squad screen.
- Praise / Criticise thumbs in the individual training list.
- Attribute progression: "Select all" / "Deselect all" buttons.

**Match, tactics and more**
- Player photos on the tactics pitch, the match pitch and the bottom bar.
- TV-style match header (score on the left).
- Calendar > Fixtures: Stadium and Attendance columns.
- Finance charts: one point per month with the value on hover.
- Page background colour of your choice (app, Page background).
